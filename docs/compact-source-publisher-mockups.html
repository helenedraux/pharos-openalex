Compact ranking-row alternatives

OPTION A

Keep the chart, tighten the row Recommended

Closest to the current visual language. Labels, bars, and values share a single 38px row.

Best continuity and easiest implementation. Long labels are truncated, so hover/focus must expose the full name.
OPTION B

Ranked compact meters

Add rank numbers, separate count from share, and reduce the bar to a small scanning aid.

Fastest to scan and almost half the current height. The shared-axis comparison becomes less explicit.
OPTION C

Dense analytical table

Treat count and share as columns; keep a minimal bar at the right for pattern recognition.

Most information-dense and strongest for exact comparison. Feels more like a data table than a publication graphic.
